Our Journey and Mission

EBBSTONE AFRIQUE LTD: 35 Years of Excellence in Agricultural Seed Production.

Established 35 years ago, EBBSTONE AFRIQUE LTD has grown to become a leading name in agricultural seed production consultation across Africa and beyond. Our journey began with a commitment to quality and to agriculture, and over the decades we have deepened our expertise in the specialized field of Agricultural Seed Production Consultation.

We pride ourselves on assisting start-up companies in seed production internationally, guiding them through every intricate step. Our extensive experience includes successful collaborations with renowned global players such as Goldsmith Seeds in California, Syngenta BV in the Netherlands, Sakata Seeds in Japan, Continental Seeds in Kenya, Bayer Cropscience in the Netherlands, and Namdhari Seed Africa. These partnerships have led to the successful establishment of seed production sites in key African markets including Kenya, Zambia, and Ethiopia.
