HERB VALUE CHAIN

From Herb Field to Export Shelf

Beyond seed, we guide growers and companies through every stage of the herb value chain: producing a consistent crop, protecting its quality after harvest, meeting the standards buyers demand, and getting it to market.

STEP 01

Herb Production

We help you plan and run herb farms that deliver steady volumes of culinary and aromatic herbs such as basil, mint, coriander, parsley, dill, chives, thyme and rosemary.

Crop and variety selection matched to your target market and climate

Land preparation, irrigation and water-quality planning

Integrated pest and disease management with traceable input records

Planting schedules and harvest calendars for consistent weekly volumes

Farm team and outgrower training in hygiene and good agricultural practice

STEP 02

Post-Harvest Handling

Fresh herbs are highly perishable, so quality is won or lost in the hours after harvest. We set up handling systems that keep product fresh, clean and market-ready.

Harvest timing and cutting methods that protect leaf quality

Shaded, rapid transfer from field to packhouse

Sorting, grading, cleaning and packhouse hygiene

Pre-cooling and cold-chain management

Packaging, labelling and lot traceability

STEP 03

Compliance & Certification

Export buyers expect proof of safe, responsible production. We prepare your farm and packhouse to meet those expectations and to pass audits with confidence.

Audit readiness for standards such as GLOBALG.A.P. and buyer-specific requirements

HACCP-based food safety systems and worker hygiene protocols

Pesticide-use records aligned with destination-market residue limits

Registration and inspection requirements with local regulators, such as KEPHIS in Kenya

Traceability systems, documentation and internal audit routines

STEP 04

Export

We take the paperwork and logistics out of exporting, so your herbs reach importers on time and in the condition the buyer expects.

Buyer identification and product specifications matched to each market

Phytosanitary certificates and export permits

Export documentation, invoicing and customs clearance

Air and sea freight planning with cold-chain handover

Coordination with importers and freight partners
